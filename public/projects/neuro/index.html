Why Neuro One

Why families choose Neuro One

An independent centre for comprehensive brain, spine and nerve care, built around evidence-based treatment delivered with a human touch.

  • Multidisciplinary team that reviews complex cases together
  • Speciality-trained nurses, technicians and rehabilitation therapists
  • Minimally invasive and endoscopic surgery options
  • 24x7 emergency and neuro intensive care
  • NABH accreditation and a clear quality and safety programme
  • Insurance and cashless support at the front desk
